Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 15, 2026 • Last Updated: September 15, 2026
Our Core Privacy Commitment

Better Twitter collects, transmits, sells, and shares ZERO user data. We believe your timeline, reading preferences, and bookmarks belong exclusively to you. The extension operates entirely within your browser offline, communicates with no external telemetry or tracking servers, and contains zero remote code.

1. Zero Data Collection & Transmission

Better Twitter does not collect any personal data, usage analytics, or browsing habits. Our architecture is deliberately designed so that no user data ever leaves your computer.

No Personally Identifiable Information (PII)

We do not collect names, email addresses, handles, passwords, IP addresses, or device fingerprints. You are completely anonymous.

No Browsing History or Activity Tracking

We do not monitor the URLs you visit, the accounts you view, or your activity outside or inside X. What you browse stays yours.

No Tweet or Content Harvesting

Your tweets, replies, likes, bookmarks, and direct messages are never harvested, scraped, logged, or processed by any external service.

Zero External Network Calls

The extension makes zero outbound network requests. No background pings, no license checks, and zero third-party telemetry beacons.

2. Local Browser Storage Only

Any data required for Better Twitter to operate is stored strictly on your local machine using standard Chrome WebExtension storage (chrome.storage.local):

  • ✔
    UI Toggles & Preferences: Your selected settings for hiding promoted posts, sidebar modules, metrics, and default feed tab preferences.
  • ✔
    Themes & Visual Customization: Your selected theme (e.g. Dracula, Nord, Matrix, Minimal) and personalized hex accent color.
  • ✔
    Local Bookmark Hub: Any custom tags, folders, or local bookmark cache you create are stored purely within your local browser sandbox and never synced to cloud servers.
  • ✔
    Twemoji Reaction Preferences: Your customized quick reaction emojis.
Permanent Deletion on Uninstall

When you uninstall Better Twitter or clear your browser's extension data, all stored local preferences and bookmark metadata are immediately and permanently destroyed by your browser.

3. Scoped Permissions & Zero Remote Code

Better Twitter strictly adheres to the principle of least privilege, complying with Google's Manifest V3 security requirements:

Permission / Attribute Configuration Purpose
permissions ['storage'] Persists your theme and UI toggle preferences locally via chrome.storage.local.
Host Permissions Zero host permissions No broad permissions (no <all_urls>, no wildcards). Zero access to any website outside X.
Content Script Matches *://x.com/*, *://twitter.com/* Scoped strictly to inject stylesheets and DOM filters onto X web pages.
Remote Code None (100% Bundled) Zero dynamic eval(), zero external CDN scripts, and zero remote module fetching.
